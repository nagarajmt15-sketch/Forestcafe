The Soil & Sprout

Our journey begins in organic shade nurseries, high in the Kalrayan mist.

Nutrient-dense compost and cool mountain air coax the first tender shoots through the soil.

— no chemicals, just patience.

Cherry Ripening

Under native canopy trees, the branches slowly fill with vibrant ruby-red cherries.

Each one is hand-picked at its peak, the way it's always been done here.

The Artisanal Roast

Pulped, sun-dried, then roasted in small batches over careful heat.

Dark chocolate, toasted caramel, deep nutty warmth — the roast finds its own character.

— small batches, watched by hand.

OUR STORY

FROM A SMALL FARM
TO THE FOREST

Our story started with a simple dream — to build something of our own, close to nature.

After leaving Chennai, we bought 2.5 acres of land in a quiet village and started our own farm. We slowly grew coffee, pepper, banana, coconut, fruits, spices and silver oak trees.

With time, the small farm became something more.

FROM THE FARM TO THE CAFE

In 2021, we opened The Forest Cafe in the beautiful Kalrayan Hills.

We wanted to bring the taste of our farm to the people who visited us. Our coffee, Podi Chai and honey became some of the special things we shared.

Our honey comes from the beehives on our own farm. Our coffee and Podi Chai are made with the same care and love that went into growing our farm.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE STORY

Behind this journey is an Art Director, Artist and Creator who has always loved creating new things.

He has worked in films and advertising, including projects such as Jeeva and Vennila Kabadi Kuzhu, and has worked on more than 500 advertisements.

For him, creativity is not only about films or advertising. He also loves seeing old and unused things and finding new ways to make them useful and beautiful.

That same creativity helped turn a small piece of land into a farm, and the farm into The Forest Cafe.

Today, The Forest Cafe is more than just a cafe.

It is our journey — from Chennai to the farm, from the farm to the forest, and from a simple idea to something real.

And our story is still growing.
